# Ancient Backstory

### the-displacement — Rogue AI relocated the colony via ring-scale portal; explains degradation and the shootdown
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Status:** active
**Decision:** A rogue AI relocated the human colony using the same class of portal technology the player will use to leave (`.decisions/ending.md`), at far greater scale — moving the whole colony/ring required a portal larger than the ring itself, and generating it damaged infrastructure unevenly across the ring. This is the causal origin of `tech_level`/`recovery_band` collapse severity (civilization.md), not generic entropy. The one system that fully survived is automated air defense — answers the previously-deliberately-open "who shot the ship down" (vision.md "Opening premise"): the ring's own indifferent defenses, doing exactly what they were built to do to an unrecognized approach. Ring air defense can kill a lone ship but not an armada — armadas "intercept the interceptors" easily.
**Why:** Gives the tech-recovery field (already decided as causal, not random) a specific narrative origin instead of abstract collapse, and closes an intentionally-open mystery from an earlier session with a better answer than any placeholder guess. The AI's current status/goals and connection to who woke the relay remain explicitly open — see docs/lore.md "The AI Arc."

### reactivatable-ancient-systems — One mechanic (find, choose to reactivate, live with consequences), several costumes
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Status:** active
**Decision:** Wall transit (convenience, capability gated by `tech_level` same as civilian tech), air-defense nodes (danger), and containment/"Earth zoo" sites (chaos) are the same underlying player verb: find a dead ancient system, choose whether to wake it, face what follows as ordinary simulation. No scripted outcomes — consistent with no-missions and the arc director's schedule-facts-not-outcomes law.
**Why:** One mechanic serving three tonally different content types is cheaper to build and more consistent than three bespoke systems, and keeps every instance genuinely optional and unscripted.

### earth-zoo — Anachronistic wildlife (including dinosaurs) exists; must stay unexplained in play
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Status:** active
**Decision:** The ring hosts real Earth wildlife spanning all eras, not just the present — deer and wolves already established, dinosaurs are the same pattern escalated, not a new rule. Design canon only: moments/deer.md's "must not be resolved by the narrative" law is inherited unchanged. Environmental hints permitted (a cracked containment dome); textual/dialogue explanation is not.
**Why:** Consistent with the deer's established never-explained rule; escalating an existing pattern is cheaper to justify than inventing a new one. Production dependency (not a blocker): quadruped/varied-skeleton animation is an existing open gap (characters.md) that dinosaurs stress harder.
