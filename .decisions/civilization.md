# Civilization / City Representation

### building-interiors-real-not-pocket — Ordinary building interiors are real, physically continuous geometry
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Status:** active
**Decision:** Regular building interiors (houses, ordinary city structures) are NOT pocket-reality swaps — they exist as real geometry continuous with the exterior, in the same coordinate space, simultaneously simulatable. Enables shooting through a window from either side and fire propagating from an interior to visibly consume the exterior structure. The pocket-reality/occlusion-funnel/spatial-lie technique remains the right tool specifically for underground dungeons, which want to feel bigger than their entrance suggests and have no need for ext/int visual continuity.
**Why:** Window combat and visible fire spread both require the interior to genuinely be part of the same world the exterior is in — a disconnected pocket dimension can't be shot into or seen burning from outside. Cost stays bounded by the same Fern-Rule split already used for city affordability: most buildings remain non-enterable shells (Tier 0–1); only a small number get real interiors, reserved for where the game actually wants that interaction.
**Caveat 2026-07-22:** flagged soft — pocket-reality for ordinary buildings may return once macro settlement/city design (how many buildings, how dense, how many need real interiors at all) is actually sketched. Sequencing note, not a reversal: macro shape should be decided before this micro technique is locked further.
