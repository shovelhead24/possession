# Knowledge / Map / Journal

### no-omniscient-map — The map renders the knowledge pyramid; the night sky is the world map
**Date:** 2026-07-19
**Status:** active
**Decision:** No omniscient map screen. The map UI renders the per-player knowledge pyramid (K1 sky-sketch silhouettes → K2 rumor claims with provenance → K3 fragments → K4 timestamped direct observation, aging visibly). Coarse remote knowledge is acquired by looking at the ring itself — inside the cylinder there is no horizon, the air is the horizon, and night lights punch through haze. Pinning = pointing at the sky.
**Why:** On a ring the geometry already shows every player the world's macro-shape — an omniscient map would duplicate the sky while deleting its purpose. Knowledge-as-map makes exploration literally "reading the world" (the original, most compelling framing of coarseness) and gives co-op asymmetric knowledge for free. NOTE: ratified 2026-07-19 ahead of any journal UI sketch, by explicit user choice over a hold recommendation — if the UI can't be made legible, supersede this rather than quietly adding a world map.
