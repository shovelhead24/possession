# Campaign design

### authored-theatre — Simple planning, tactical run and gun, bounded strategic agency
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Status:** active
**Decision:** The core is ring-scale tactical run and gun following simple mission planning. The theatre is a highly authored story, with permitted local changes and strategic route choices inside defined story bounds. Branching sector/operation graphs are allowed. Captured relay fortresses become FOBs and extend coordinated support: no comms, no bombs. Detailed theatre timing, unsupported travel, recapture and mission failure remain open.
**Why:** The RTS-derived organisation provides readable situations and consequences for direct combat. A medieval-first progression and broad RPG/tech stack are not prerequisites for proving the original conception.
**Supersedes/amends:** `design-laws.md#no-authored-graphs` no longer forbids campaign routes or authored theatre structure. `chosen-commitments` no longer rules out a mission-planning layer; story waiting/clock policy is open. `principles-ratified` must not be used to prohibit authored encounters or visible functional rewards. Earlier independent-history wording limits player control of major events, not the author's control of their scheduling. Unrelated dialogue authoring and technical constraints remain unless explicitly changed.

### physical-information — FOB planning and a bounded companion
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Status:** active
**Decision:** Objects are the primary interface. FOBs have mission-planning maps. Maps depict available knowledge, not omniscient world truth. An AI companion is wanted. A stationary local hologram, translation and specific access powers are proposals requiring tests; the companion's identity and narrative centrality remain open. Fictional AI does not change the no-runtime-LLM rule.
**Why:** The world and communications network make both tactical and strategic information usable. A display cannot grant information its owner lacks.
**Supersedes/amends:** `knowledge.md#no-omniscient-map` retains imperfect knowledge but no longer makes the sky the exclusive world-map interface. The sky remains an orientation cue. See `docs/information-and-companion.md`.

### prototype-before-catalogue — First operation determines asset needs
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Status:** active
**Decision:** Pause the catalogue-wide model worker. Build and assess the proposed FOB/map/relay operation using greyboxes and existing assets before commissioning broad production art. Retain existing outputs and backlog as history. Combat values and physical asset sizes remain provisional; attack reach is not mesh length. Theatrics remains a title candidate; no repository/folder rename is authorised.
**Why:** The current model checks cannot establish a playable hologram map, mission logic or combat suitability. Earlier exploration-only slice and tech-tier assumptions are deferred from immediate delivery scope.
