# Performance Demo floating origin — 2026-10-24

Enabled by default in Performance Demo. `--no-floating-origin` disables automatic shifts for diagnostic comparisons. Other demo scenes do not install the service.

## Coordinate contract

- `terrain_drive_ring.gd`: gameplay positions are local simulation coordinates. `Ring.point()` converts persistent arc/height/lateral coordinates into local positions; `Ring.coordinates()` performs the inverse. The Cartesian origin offset is accumulated as three scalar doubles. The existing Vector3 return of `coordinates()` retains its existing float32 ring-coordinate precision.
- `ring_frame.gd`: immutable, original ring coordinates. Cached terrain vertices and cloud chunk resources must use this frame, independent of the current simulation origin. Terrain cuts and streaming partitioning also operate in this original frame.
- Terrain meshes and collision bodies published from cached data receive `-Ring.offset()` at publication, including worker results that finish after a shift. Never bake the live origin into disk caches.
- Persistent construction, actor, tree, terrain and player-start formats retain their existing meanings. Do not apply the simulation offset to serialized ring coordinates.

## Shifts

`performance_floating_origin.gd` runs at physics priority -1000. When the focus is more than 2048 m from local zero, it subtracts a rounded Cartesian displacement from spatial roots. It uses the vehicle/player during play and the camera during viewpoint tours or editor flight. Shifts wait while asynchronous landing resolution or vehicle relocation is active.

Translation does not rotate the frame or alter velocity. Rigid-body linear/angular velocity is retained, and interpolation is reset. Spatial children move with their root exactly once; identity organizational containers stay at identity so future spawns use the same local frame. Untouched node anchors are retained in scalar doubles to avoid accumulating rounding on return trips. A Node3D with model-relative children that may start at identity should set `floating_origin_root` metadata (see actor previews and rain).

Scripts retaining local-space points implement `origin_shifted(delta)`. Examples include landing-region centres, suspension contacts, on-foot safe positions, actor snapshots/routes, AI goals, projectiles and editor guides. Do not translate directions, velocities, local bone offsets, or persistent ring coordinates. New systems must observe this distinction.

Tree/construction batches keep their already-shifted visual nodes until their normal refresh budget uploads the translated instance poses. They reset the batch transform at that upload; geometry is not rebuilt inside the shift callback. Terrain geometry is not regenerated by a shift.

## Rendering and diagnostics

`ring_origin_offset` is a global shader uniform with zero default. Water noise and satellite mapping preserve their absolute reference; cloud drift and ring-layer camera comparisons account for the offset. Cached cloud chunks use a new absolute-coordinate cache namespace. Camera-relative mist and first-person weapon rendering remain in their respective frames.

World-space CPU wheel-dust particles restart on a shift because their individual particle positions cannot be translated by this controller. The short existing dust trail may disappear; rain uses local particle coordinates and shifts with its emitter.

`FLOATING_ORIGIN` log events include offset and shift duration. Ring-position telemetry (including F8 context) includes `origin_offset`; raw physics positions are local and should be interpreted with that offset. F8 clears its previous-contact comparison at shifts to avoid reporting the translation as suspension movement.

## Validation

- `game/benchmarks/test_floating_origin.gd`: coordinates, unchanged serialized placement record, velocity/orientation, camera-child hierarchy, millimetre motion, repeated shifts.
- `profile_performance_weather.gd --floating-full-review`: isolated full-scene tests for automatic remote viewpoint travel, player spawn, saved-start write/load/application, return home, tree-body alignment and unchanged tree/construction/actor records; then stationary/driving comparisons on near/distant flat pads with a forced shift halfway through each driving case.
- `--floating-visual-review`: water before/after and legacy/ring/painterly cloud modes after shifting.
- Always use all four isolated save overrides. The feature test writes its isolated player-start sidecar; use a fresh fixture directory for reproducibility.
- Existing `--flat-car-review` and `--car-frame-review` disable automatic shifting within their cases; also launch with `--no-floating-origin` and a clean isolated player-start sidecar for historical comparisons.
