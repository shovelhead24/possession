# Possession — Macro Geography Sketch

Area doc (2026-07-22 generation round, PROPOSED throughout — this is the "macro first" pass the interiors question was waiting on). Positions given as % of arc from the crash region (lon 0 by convention); all names deferred; all figures sketch-grade, not tuning values.

## The Arc, Walked Spinward

- **0% — Crash region.** The first-slice geography, now placeable: river-valley + conifer night-forest edge (the two slice biomes, biomes.md), the deer meadow, the reveal ridge, a crater-lake syncopation within a day's walk. Regressed band beyond the forest — the first people found are the most fallen.
- **~3–8% — THE city (and Protagonist Two's start).** Close enough that its death is *witnessable* from the crash region's ridge line (city_destruction.md needs P1 to see lights go out). **Backprop flag → co-op/D5:** if both players share one origin frame (substrate.md), the two starts must be within tether range *or* the per-viewport frame problem gets solved first — the narrative geography and the rendering architecture are now explicitly coupled; recorded in co-op.md.
- **~10–15% — Ford-town corridor.** The frontier band's heartland: river crossings, the truck-and-saloon belt, first real factions chain.
- **~18–25% — The sea.** Delta marsh at the inflow (boats become necessary, not optional), lagoon coast (flamingos, RIBs), drowned towns at the old shoreline, lighthouse chain along it. Real-DEM grounding candidates verified: `mizen_head`, `loop_head` (terrain/splice-portfolio.md) — both carry real lighthouse/signal-station infrastructure on site.
- **~30% — Alloy barrens + portal-anchor scar.** The mid-game wall: the Displacement's scar as a traversal filter — you cross it changed or you turn back. The one biome that explains the whole world to anyone paying attention, silently.
- **~40–55% — The enclave.** Far enough that reaching it is an act, near enough that its lights dominate a whole quadrant of the night sky from the crash region — **the K1 lights you mark on night one are the enclave; the game's first self-authored goal is baked into the sky itself.** Desert (salvage paradox) on its rain-shadow flank; the surviving city's economy question (civilization.md) lives here and remains open.
- **~60–75% — Highland/wall country.** The "climb toward ancient tech" stretch: wall-breach stairs, glider updrafts, the relay somewhere high (relay.md — already humming). Pinch-heavy: the medieval-army moment's natural home. Real-DEM grounding candidate verified: `priests_leap` (terrain/splice-portfolio.md) — a genuine mountain-pass pinch point.
- **~80% — Containment dome / lost-world pocket** (optional, off-mainline — deferrable content by placement, per the dome's production note).
- **~90–100% — Approach to the hub spire.** The portal ascent (the_return.md): the last stretch of arc is the most degraded *and* the most ancient — the end of the world is the beginning of it.

## Reading the Layout

- The arc is a difficulty/knowledge gradient *by geography, not gates*: everything is walkable day one (explorability law), but knowledge, tools, and transport determine what walking *means*.
- Optional content (dome, deep sea) hangs off the mainline — production can defer it without holes in the spine.
- Anti-spinward from the crash (the "other way") stays deliberately under-specified this round: co-op divergence space, second-playthrough space, or DLC space. Not empty — unwritten.
