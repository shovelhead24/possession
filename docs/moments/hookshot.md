# The Hookshot

## What You See
Ancient alien tech. You pick it up. You fire it across a canyon. The world snaps toward you — or you toward it. The scale of everything you've been walking through suddenly becomes traversable in a different way.

## What You Feel
The game changed. Not the world — you. This is the moment the ring stops being a place you walk across and starts being a place you move through.

## Mechanic
Grapple/hookshot. Fires at geometry, pulls player or swings. First use should be across something that would take minutes to walk around. The contrast is the lesson.

## What It Must Do
- Be found late enough that the player has felt the ring's scale on foot first.
- Work on the first try — the first swing should feel good.
- Open up previously visible but unreachable geometry retroactively.

## What It Must Not Do
- Be explained with a tutorial.
- Be the first piece of ancient tech — it should feel like a progression, not an introduction.
- Break on potato hardware.
