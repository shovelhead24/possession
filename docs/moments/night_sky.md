# The Night Sky

## What You See
At night, you look up. The other side of the ring arcs overhead — terrain, ocean, clouds. And light. Clusters of it. Cities. Towns. A civilisation on the other side, close enough to see, impossibly far to reach on foot. Just standing there, looking up.

## What You Feel
You are not where the action is. There is somewhere to go. The ring is inhabited and you are in the wilderness.

## Mechanic
None. You stop. You look up. The game does nothing.

## What It Must Do
- Be geometrically honest — the far side is visible because the ring curves, not because of a skybox.
- Make the lights readable as cities from a distance.
- Back up the promise — the lights are reachable. If you walk far enough, you get there.
- Let the lights go dark in the city destruction moment, visible from here.

## What It Must Not Do
- Use a skybox trick that breaks if the player looks carefully.
- Show a city that can't be visited.
- Annotate or label the lights.
