# The Ring Curve

## What You See
You crest a ridge on foot. The terrain ahead curves upward. Mountains above you. Ocean above that. The other side of the ring hanging in the sky like a painting. No prompt. No music.

## What You Feel
Disorientation, then awe. The world is wrong in a way that is beautiful. You are standing on the inside of something.

## Mechanic
None. You walk. You see it. The game does nothing else.

> **Amended 2026-07-18** (see `.decisions/world.md#cosmology-cheated`): the *near-field* curve the player walks on stays geometrically real; the distant upward sweep is stylized/exaggerated, since the honest curve at 20,000 km circumference is too subtle to read.

## What It Must Do
- Hit without fanfare. No sting, no UI callout.
- Be geometrically correct — the ring actually curves, this isn't a skybox trick.
- Reward players who explore rather than follow a path.

## What It Must Not Do
- Announce itself.
- Be the first thing the player sees — it must be discovered.
- Use a cutscene.
