# The Deer

## What You See
Before the wolves — deer. Normal deer. On an alien ring.

## What You Feel
A question that sits with you. Something brought them here, or something made them. The game never answers it.

## Mechanic
None. They're just there.

## What It Must Do
- Appear before the wolves, so the wolves feel like a corruption of something peaceful.
- Be recognisably normal — not alien deer, not strange deer. Deer.
- Never be explained.

## What It Must Not Do
- Draw attention to itself.
- Be interactive in a meaningful way.
- Be resolved by the narrative.
