# The Wolves

## What You See
Night. Movement at the edge of the treeline. Then more movement. Then running.

## What You Feel
You are prey. This is an alien ring and you are not the apex predator. You run into the darkness like a mouse.

## Mechanic
The wolves are faster than you. You cannot fight them effectively — not yet, not with what you have. The ridge and the trees are the solution. The terrain saves you, not your weapon.

## What It Must Do
- Establish early that the carbine does not solve everything.
- Make the player feel small and hunted.
- Resolve through terrain navigation, not combat.
- Happen at night — darkness matters.

## What It Must Not Do
- Give the player a clean fight.
- Explain the wolves.
- Feel like a tutorial.
