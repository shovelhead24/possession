# The City Destruction

## What You See
Protagonist Two. She left the city for her own reasons — before it happens. She turns around and watches. The city, then not the city. No music swell. No close calls. Cold and distant, like the T2 nuclear dream sequence.

Meanwhile, on the far side of the ring, Protagonist One sees a flicker of lights go dark. No context. No explanation.

## What You Feel
For Protagonist Two: helplessness, not heroism. The disaster doesn't care that she's there.
For Protagonist One: something changed, somewhere far away. They don't know what yet.

## Mechanic
Protagonist Two watches from a distance — not an escape sequence. She already left. She just happens to be facing the wrong direction when it happens.

The couch co-op beat: the player who played her knows exactly what that distant flicker was. The player who didn't is still finding out.

## What It Must Do
- Show the destruction from a distance. Not inside it, not running from it.
- Be cold. No heroism, no last-second escapes.
- Let Protagonist One's version be ambiguous — just lights going out.
- Make the shared knowledge between co-op players feel like grief.

## What It Must Not Do
- Give Protagonist Two an escape sequence.
- Use the destruction as spectacle for its own sake.
- Explain what destroyed it immediately.
