# The Settlement at Night

## What You See
Firelight before people. You approach from outside the circle of light. You can see them — medieval, armed, alive — before they see you. You choose when to step in.

## What You Feel
Relief and caution at the same time. You are not alone on the ring. But you don't know yet if that's good.

## Mechanic
The player has agency over the moment of contact. Step into the light when ready. They go quiet when you do.

## What It Must Do
- Let the player observe before being observed.
- Make the silence when they notice you land.
- Establish that the ring has a history you weren't part of.

## What It Must Not Do
- Rush the player into contact.
- Use a cutscene for the approach.
- Explain who these people are immediately.
