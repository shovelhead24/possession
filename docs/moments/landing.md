# The Landing

## What You See
The ring from above, coming in fast. The curve of it. The terrain rushing up. The pilot's hands on a dead stick. Through the viewport the whole way down — no cuts, no fades.

## What You Feel
Scale before anything else. You understand where you are before you're told. The ring is enormous and you are very small and it does not care that you're falling toward it.

## Mechanic
Mouse look only. No thrust, no control. The ship is dying — you're fighting to keep the nose up, buying a survivable angle. You are present, you are doing something, and you are losing.

## What It Must Do
- Run unbroken from atmosphere to impact. No loading screen, no cut.
- Teach mouse look before the player knows they're being taught it.
- Let the player choose what to look at on the way down — horizon, terrain, the ring curving away sideways.
- Make every player's memory of the landing slightly different.

## What It Must Not Do
- Cut away at the moment of impact.
- Show a health bar, a HUD, a tutorial prompt.
- Let the player feel in control.
- Use music to tell the player how to feel.
