# The Pilot

## What You See
Day two. You find the cockpit — it separated on impact. The pilot is still strapped in, visor down. They left something on the seat. You don't know what it means yet. You won't for a long time.

## What You Feel
You were not alone on that ship. And now you are.

## Mechanic
You can pick up what they left. It goes into inventory without explanation. Its purpose becomes clear much later — or never, if you don't reach that point.

## What It Must Do
- Be found on day two, not day one. Give the player time to be alone first.
- Keep the pilot anonymous — visor down, no name yet.
- Leave the object unexplained at the time of discovery.

## What It Must Not Do
- Show the pilot's face.
- Explain the object.
- Make this a quest trigger.
