# The Human Armada (post-credits)

Companion to [The Return](the_return.md) — the leave ending's stinger. **Not** [The Armada](armada.md) (moment 9, mid-game): that one is an unrelated ancient civilization, indifferent to the player, arriving because someone used ring tech to call home. This one exists *because of* the player. 2026-07-22 session; status tentative — timing may move earlier than post-credits if a future pass needs it; deliberately built as a content-deferral device (sequel/DLC hook), not built out now.

## What You See

Credits end. One more beat: the humans of the player's own world arrive at the ring, in force — following the signal the player's own return created.

## What You Feel

The rhyme lands: the game opened with the player answering someone else's distress call and getting shot down for it (vision.md "Opening premise"). Now the player's own people are the ones answering a call — arriving the same way every armada in this world arrives: indifferent to the ring's inhabitants, wanting something, unaware of (or unconcerned with) the cost of asking.

## Mechanic

None — this is a tease, not a playable beat, in v0. Whether it becomes the cold open of a sequel/DLC, a played-out epilogue, or stays a single held shot is undecided.

## What It Must Do

- Read as caused by the player specifically, not as another instance of "The Armada" recurring.
- Stay small in v0 — a tease, not new content to build now.

## What It Must Not Do

- Be confused in-doc or in-game with moment 9's armada — keep the naming and the causality distinct.
- Grow scope before it's actually time to build the sequel/DLC content it's deferring.
