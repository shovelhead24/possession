# Possession — Faction Repertoire

Curated vocabulary of what a settlement can *do* — the outcomes a player predicts, and the things
that later return to them as consequence. Started 2026-07-30.

**Method: trust the sparse meat-space model.** These are not invented game verbs. They are what
small, isolated, subsistence communities under scarcity actually do — because that is the target,
and the real thing is both richer and cheaper than anything designed from scratch. Where a real
behaviour exists, use it.

**Scale assumption:** settlements of tens to a few hundred people, days apart, on a chain
(`factions.md`). At that scale *demography is structural* — one death is a political event, and a
marriage is foreign policy.

Curated first, **independent of what signals would reveal them**. Signal design comes after; getting
the vocabulary interesting is the harder and more important half.

---

## The year's work — subsistence rhythm

The baseline pulse. Boring individually, load-bearing collectively: everything else is read as
*deviation* from this (see M3 findings).

- **Move the herds up** to summer grazing — transhumance, a big visible movement on a known schedule
- **Bring the herds down** early — the single most legible fear signal a pastoral people has
- **Plant** / **harvest** — fixes everyone in place for a window; nobody raids during their own harvest
- **Cut and haul timber** before the wet
- **Slaughter before winter** — routine; *slaughtering early* is desperation
- **Repair the roofs / the ditch / the mill**

## Cattle and theft — the classic sparse-society engine

Historically the dominant form of inter-community violence in exactly this kind of landscape (Irish
*táin*, Scottish reiving, Nuer cattle raids). **Strongest cluster in the list**: it is economic,
reciprocal, endlessly repeatable, rarely fatal, and generates grievance-facts with coordinates —
which is precisely what L5's history pass wants.

- **Lift a neighbour's cattle** — theft as economy, not war
- **Reprisal raid** — the return leg, expected, almost formal
- **Track and recover** stolen stock
- **Pay a fine in cattle** to close a feud before it escalates
- **Refuse the fine** — the escalation beat
- **Hostage exchange** to guarantee a peace

## Movement of people

- **Send a caravan** spinward / antispinward
- **Send an envoy** — a person, not a message; slow, and a hostage by implication
- **Send a runner** with news
- **Take in refugees** / **turn refugees away** — the same event, opposite character
- **Evacuate the outlying farms**
- **Move the whole settlement** — rare, enormous, usually toward water or a working machine

## Fear and defence

- **Close the walls**
- **Post a watch** where there was none
- **Move stores underground / out of the settlement**
- **Burn their own fields** — denial; the point of no return
- **Abandon a route** because of what happens on it

## Force

- **Ride out** against a named neighbour
- **Garrison a pinch** — chokepoint control, the chain topology made physical
- **Call up fighting men** — visible, slow, and impossible to hide
- **Stand down / disband** — as readable as mustering, and more informative
- **Hire outsiders** — a faction that cannot fight for itself

## Demography — structural at this scale

- **A death** — and *whose*; a headman's death is a succession crisis
- **A birth**
- **A marriage** — foreign policy conducted with a daughter; the cheapest alliance mechanism a
  sparse society has, and it braids into everything
- **An exile** — someone removed, who then exists elsewhere carrying a grievance
- **A split** — the settlement fissions; two factions where there was one

## The ancient — the cosmology axis made into verbs

The distinctive cluster: nothing else in the genre has this, and it differentiates factions by
*belief* rather than by stats (`factions.md`).

- **Wall up a ruin entrance** — taboo, actively maintained
- **Leave an offering at a machine** — cargo-cult
- **Send someone in** to salvage — instrumental
- **Try to wake something** — the player's own verb, performed by someone else
- **Punish someone for touching it** — belief with teeth
- **Abandon a taboo** — the biggest cosmological event a culture can have, and it should be rare

## Talk, judgement, story

- **Hold a moot** — the settlement decides something in public
- **Hear a traveller out** — how rumor actually enters a place
- **Silence someone**
- **Put out a story deliberately** — a faction using the rumor system on purpose
- **Judge a dispute** — water rights, grazing, a boundary

## Toward the player specifically

Reputation made into action (`factions.md`: reputation is inverted knowledge, not a meter).

- **Send someone to find you**
- **Close their gates to you** in particular
- **Offer you something** — the ask that is not a quest (`factions.md` no-missions boundary)
- **Put a price on you**
- **Credit you with something you did not do** — misattribution as an *action*, not just a rumor

## Author's cut — the strongest, and why

1. **Cattle raiding and its reciprocity chain** (lift → reprisal → fine → refuse → hostage). One
   real mechanism produces an entire escalation ladder, generates located grievances for free, and
   is economic rather than apocalyptic — so it can run constantly without exhausting the world.
2. **Bring the herds down early.** The purest signal in the list: a whole community acting on fear
   before anyone has said the word. Readable at a glance, from outside, with no dialogue.
3. **Marriage as foreign policy.** Cheapest possible alliance system, entirely diegetic, and it
   braids into demography, trade, succession and war simultaneously.
4. **Abandon a taboo.** The rarest and heaviest — a culture changing its mind about the ancient.
   Should probably be a once-a-playthrough event, and the armada is the obvious cause.
5. **Credit you with something you did not do.** Misattributed reactivation promoted from a dynamic
   into a thing factions actively *do* — the possession theme wearing politics.

## Notes for signal design (deferred, but recorded)

- The subsistence rhythm is the **baseline**; everything else reads as deviation from it. That is
  the M3 run-2 model and it worked (84% vs 69% base rate).
- **No outcome may have a unique tell.** M3 run 2's defect: unique phrasing per outcome is a lookup
  table however indirect the phrasing. Every observable must be consistent with 2–3 outcomes.
- Outcomes should differ in **scale and reversibility**, not just kind — lifting cattle is Tuesday;
  burning your own fields is never coming back.
