# Possession — Wildlife

Area doc (2026-07-22 generation round, all PROPOSED). Earth-zoo canon (lore.md): real Earth wildlife, eras intermixed, never explained in-game. Design law inherited from the deer: animals are field consumers and *small-amplitude* tempo writers — fauna must never read as content dispensers.

**Artery rule, fauna edition: every species needs ≥2 systemic roles** (an ecological role + at least one player-facing interaction that isn't "shoot it").

## Catalog

- **Deer** (canonical, moments/deer.md) — prey base; the null-write peace signal; *living sensors*: a herd bolting tells you something's moving before you see it.
- **Wolves** (canonical, moments/wolves.md) — night predator, hard tempo writer; baitable — a carcass is a tool (drag a kill to redirect the pack's night route).
- **Flamingos** (the beach vignette, world.md) — spectacle; **involuntary rumor**: a flushed flock broadcasts your position to every observer in line of sight — beauty that betrays you.
- **Corvids** — carrion markers: circling birds are a free K2 claim ("something died there") visible at kilometers. Billboard-cheap.
- **Fish** — food economy (rod, band-gated); water readability: surface activity hints at what's beneath.
- **Wild horses** — transport acquisition *outside* the tech tree: taming is a living alternative to finding a truck. The frontier band's tech and its fauna compete.
- **Boar** — aggressive-neutral danger (not a predator, just won't be pushed); **pressure writer**: boars raid crops → settlement hunger pressure rises → hunting parties form → encounters exist because pigs are hungry. Fauna feeding R1 directly.
- **Goats/sheep (domestic)** — walking currency; rustling as a faction-friction verb the player can join, prevent, or suffer.
- **Dinosaurs** (Earth-zoo escalation) — three uses, ranked by cost-realism: a **sauropod herd** as walking scale-without-fanfare (the deer rule at 100× — seen at distance, never explained, possibly never approachable); **raptor pack** as the wolves' tier-2/3 echo (same code shape, higher stakes); a **lost-world pocket** behind a cracked containment dome where the zoo never broke (optional region — see locations.md). Rig cost flagged: stresses the existing quadruped gap (characters.md) hardest of anything in the game.

## Ranking — author's cut

1. **Boar as pressure writer** — fauna generating politics through the existing R-stack; encounters nobody authored.
2. **Flamingos as involuntary rumor** — one flock turns stealth, beauty, and knowledge physics into a single mechanic.
3. **Corvids as free K2** — the cheapest knowledge feature in the game; three sprites and a circle path.
4. **Wild horses vs. trucks** — the tech tree with a heartbeat; acquisition as taming story.
5. **Sauropod at distance** — pure moment fuel, but ranked below the others because it's cost without system (until the rig pipeline exists).
