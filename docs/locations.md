# Possession — Location Archetypes

Area doc (2026-07-22 generation round, all PROPOSED). Locations are where syncopations (layerbuf-v0.md), civilization fields, and lore intersect — archetypes the bake instantiates, not hand-placed setpieces. The anomaly-type registry effectively lives here now (backprop note added to layerbuf-v0.md).

## Catalog

- **Crater-lake pilgrimage town** (canonical cascade) — oasis ring in steppe, rim settlement, myth economy.
- **Alloy-scar fortress pass** (canonical cascade) — all roads through one gap; battles by geometry; the medieval-army moment's natural stage.
- **Pylon-row road towns** (canonical cascade) — civilization strung along the bones of dead transit.
- **Cracked containment dome** — the zoo's broken pen: dinosaur region boundary, environmental hint that must never be textually explained (lore.md guard rail). Optional pocket off the mainline — deferrable content by geography.
- **Portal-anchor scar** — the Displacement's ground zero: a dead zone where the bake's damage fields all point, AI fragments at their densest, late-game gravity well. The lore's one *place*.
- **Wall-breach stair** — access to wall-top transit; a climb that converts the wall from horizon-fixture to place (world.md's walls-as-destinations, concretized).
- **Ford towns** — `ford_candidates` made civic: bridges are wealth, chokepoints, and casus belli in one.
- **Drowned town** — sea-edge salvage; the sub's reason to exist; rooms fish swim through.
- **Lighthouse / beacon tower** — knowledge infrastructure: a manned light is a rumor hub and a K3 trade node — locals sell *bearings*, not maps. The knowledge economy gets architecture. Real-world grounding: `mizen_head` and `loop_head` (terrain/splice-portfolio.md) both carry genuine signal-station/lighthouse sites.
- **Battlefield ruin** — a baked grievance with coordinates (factions.md); scavengers, ghosts-by-reputation, and the reason two banners hate each other, visitable.
- **THE city (after)** — protagonist two's wound as walkable space; also the game's largest honest ruin.
- **The enclave city (living)** — still gated on the unsolved economy question (civilization.md) — listed, not designed.

## Ranking — author's cut

1. **Lighthouse as knowledge-trade node** — sells bearings, hosts rumors: the knowledge pyramid gets an economy and a building in one archetype.
2. **Portal-anchor scar** — the backstory as a location instead of a codex entry.
3. **Cracked containment dome** — show-don't-tell at its purest, and production-deferrable by placement.
4. **Ford towns** — smallest archetype, biggest systemic reach (hydrology → economy → war).
