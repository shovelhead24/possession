# Core conception — ring-scale tactical run and gun

Adopted planning baseline, 2026-09-12. This describes intended design, not implemented features. Start from [the planning index](brief.md). The repository remains Possession; Shortcut is an earlier recorded title and Theatrics is a candidate, not an approved rename.

## Decided

The core experience is simple mission planning followed by tactical run and gun across a large ring. Planning establishes an objective, approach and equipment; moment-to-moment play centres on movement, shooting and improvisation. Do not require continuous RTS micromanagement during combat.

The theatre is a highly authored story. The player can substantially affect particular fights, routes, positions and people within limits established by that story. They cannot freely rewrite its major events through an unconstrained war simulation. The scheduling of those events is unresolved; “authored” does not yet mean a real-time deadline or a trigger that waits for the player.

Communications enable coordinated fire support: no comms, no bombs. Relay fortresses extend operational reach and become FOBs. A branching sector map makes the available routes and operations understandable. Objects and their observable behaviour are the primary interface. The AI companion is wanted; translation is a proposed role.

Crude realism governs art: credible construction and proportions with economical geometry/materials. The target is not cartoonish low-poly illustration. Weapon geometry is provisional until tested against player movement and enemy behaviour. Gameplay reach does not specify physical mesh length.

## Working combat hypothesis — proposed for testing

Players read a threat and an opportunity, act through movement and weapons, provoke a legible response and adapt. An exchange should be enjoyable before its distant campaign payoff. Thirty seconds is a useful observation window, not a compulsory encounter duration. Success can include securing access or disengaging; eliminating every enemy is not a universal completion condition.

Enemy behaviour must make useful predictions possible. Consistent warning, perception, attack and recovery cues permit surprises in arrangement without arbitrary changes in rules. Health/shields, carrying limits, melee reach, enemy roster and support restrictions remain open. Halo CE is a reference for experience, not permission to silently adopt every mechanic in a pasted summary.

## Scope and inheritance

Preserve reachable scale, ordinary life, quiet, local attachment, existing technical constraints and the co-op ambition. Validate the first operation within an authored boundary before expanding world simulation. Ring dimensions, terrain architecture and lore are not changed by this baseline.

Medieval-first progression, broad tech trees, survival ladders, full RPG simulation and completing all 46 catalogue models are deferred. Earlier exploration/landing moments remain material for the game but are no longer the immediate core-loop prototype. Recovery and equipment acquisition may return when their contribution to this experience is demonstrated.

## Open decisions

- Which player/enemy movement and defence model makes the exchange enjoyable?
- Which local outcomes persist, and how does each authored beat accommodate them?
- How do failure, retries, co-op separation and campaign time work?
- What makes the companion a character, and how important is it to the story?
- Does Theatrics name the intended experience better than the existing titles?

Resolve these through [one operation](first-operation.md), not a larger asset catalogue.
