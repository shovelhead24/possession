# Campaign and player agency

Baseline adopted 2026-09-12. See [core conception](core-conception.md).

## Authority at three scales

| Scale | Decided direction | Boundary / unresolved detail |
|---|---|---|
| Tactical | Player movement, targeting and improvisation alter the fight. | Enemy types, exact combat values and failure rules require prototypes. |
| Strategic | Simple mission planning; branching ring sectors; relay fortresses become FOBs and extend support. | Route costs, local persistence, recapture and supply depth are open. |
| Theatre | Major story events and their overall shape are highly authored. | Timing, allowable reordering, triggers and permitted deviations are open. |

An RTS-derived organisation of forces can supply understandable local situations. It does not author an alternative theatre history behind the player's back. Procedural systems arrange terrain, units and local circumstances inside explicit authored limits. Campaign route graphs are now permitted; the earlier categorical ban on authored graphs does not apply to this layer.

## Communications and routes

No comms, no bombs: coordinated fire support requires a working communications path. Capturing and activating a relay fortress extends the operational network and creates a new planning/departure base. The map represents that branching network on actual ring sectors.

This is the operational advancement rule. Whether the player can physically venture outside coverage, and what unsupported travel permits, must be decided explicitly; no invisible wall or guaranteed bypass is implied. For the prototype, the frontier relay must be capturable with currently available means. Do not create a circular requirement where capturing the relay requires support that only that relay can enable.

Proposed relay states: hostile, secured but offline, linking, operational. Show these through the antenna, console, staff and radio acknowledgements. An operational relay and an available support asset are separate facts: communications permit coordination, not unlimited ammunition or instantaneous attacks. Support range, delay and availability are prototype tuning questions.

## Simple planning

At the FOB, choose an operation/route, inspect known opposition, choose kit and transport, and optionally give allies one simple job. Show why a route is available or unsupported through the map's links and local equipment. Extensive unit orders, economics and tech research are outside the first operation.

A proposed local consequence is a captured fortress remaining a friendly departure point. An authored theatre event may still occur afterwards. The story must acknowledge the supported local outcomes instead of silently deleting the player's success.

## Authoring contract to complete per story beat

Record: the event that must occur; what the player may change; prerequisites; how its approach is signalled; allowed timing candidates; interrupted/failed/co-op cases; and what persists afterwards. Until timing is decided, do not implement an unstoppable clock or promise that the story will always wait.

Example prototype aftermath: an authenticated transmission reveals the next authored situation and two candidate routes. This is test staging, not final plot or a ratified trigger policy. Relay loss, chain collapse and defence chores are not commissioned by this document.
