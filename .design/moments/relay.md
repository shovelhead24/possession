# The Relay

## What You See
An ancient structure. You arrive. It's already on. Humming. Something woke it before you got here. You didn't do this.

## What You Feel
You are not the protagonist of this story. You are a latecomer. The ring has its own business.

## Mechanic
You can interact with it. But the first thing you notice is that it was already running. The interaction options only make sense later.

## What It Must Do
- Be clearly active before the player touches anything.
- Raise the question of who woke it without answering it.
- Feel old — ancient, not broken, not abandoned. Running.

## What It Must Not Do
- Wait for the player to activate it.
- Explain itself.
- Feel like a quest objective.
