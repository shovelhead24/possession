# The Medieval Army

## What You See
You're on a ridge. Below, two factions fight. A real battle — not a skirmish, not a scripted event. It was happening before you arrived. It will resolve without you if you walk away.

## What You Feel
The ring has politics. History. People who have been fighting long before you crashed here. You are an observer, not the arbitrator.

## Mechanic
You can intervene or not. The battle's outcome changes either way, but the world doesn't collapse if you do nothing. It already has momentum.

## What It Must Do
- Be visible from the ridge before the player descends — give them time to watch.
- Run on actual AI, not a scripted sequence. The outcome can vary.
- Feel like it interrupted something larger.

## What It Must Not Do
- Wait for the player to arrive before starting.
- Require intervention.
- Explain which side is right.
