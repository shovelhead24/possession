# Animated tank tracks and traction

The Altay uses its original 62 links per side, instanced around the original closed track paths. Road wheels, idlers and sprockets rotate with their side's belt speed. Left and right belts run independently, including opposite directions for stationary pivot turns. W/S drive forwards/backwards, A/D steer, Space brakes.

Eight vertical ground rays per side align with the road wheels and leading run. Each applies a Hooke-law spring and vertical damping term; contact positions also make a left/right and front/rear support plane for pitch and roll, avoiding feedback from the previous hull tilt. A broad physical hull collider meets terrain, ridges and walls. Motion is direct hull-relative track control: W=(forward, forward), S=(reverse, reverse), A=(reverse, forward), D=(forward, reverse). Space and neutral input both stop horizontal motion without commanding reverse. High lateral grip makes low-speed direction changes bite immediately; deliberate skid only appears at high speed. This is a contact-based arcade drivetrain, not articulated track-link physics or individual wheel suspension. Links follow their fixed model path rather than deforming around terrain.

`test_tank_tracks.gd` checks both sets of links, forward/reverse motion, brakes, opposed-track pivots, loss of propulsion and steering in the air, and independent contact on a narrow platform. Run with `-- --pictures` for rendered inspection captures.
